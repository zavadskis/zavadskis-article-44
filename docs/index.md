# Andrejs Zavadskis: The Commercial and Portrait Photographer Behind Forbes Billionaire Covers, Gumball 3000 Convoys and the Dakar Rally

European photographer based in Riga, Latvia. Portraits and commercial work since 2004, in more than fifty countries — and the same rule everywhere: the moment does not repeat.

Prague, April 2025. A billionaire, a few minutes between meetings, and a Forbes editor waiting for a portrait strong enough to open a story.

On a Gumball 3000 route, the convoy roars past — supercars, flashes, crowds at every stop. No second take. He has ridden along five times.

Black Rock Desert at night. Dust in the air, fire on the horizon, and a camera that has to see in the dark.

This is not a biography. It is a handful of frames, and the photographer who keeps ending up inside them: Andrejs Zavadskis, often called Latvia's most popular photographer.

## The portrait desk

Clients include Forbes USA, Nike, airBaltic, LMT, Rimi, Nordea, Swedbank and Tele2. A business portrait is a quiet kind of pressure: limited time, a person used to being in charge, and one frame that has to carry the headline.

- [Paweł Chudziński, Point Nine Capital](https://www.forbes.com/sites/iainmartin/2024/12/12/europes-top-vc-bet-big-on-berlins-once-poor-but-sexy-startup-scene/) — the Forbes "Europe's Top VC" cover, later used for the Midas List Europe 2024.
- [Thomas Plantenga, Vinted](https://www.forbes.com/sites/iainmartin/2024/04/15/how-lithuanian-startup-vinted-spun-secondhand-clothes-sales-into-gold/) — Forbes USA, April 2024, "Riches from Rags".
- [Remon Vos, CTP](https://www.forbes.com/sites/giacomotognini/2025/04/13/meet-the-billionaire-whose-european-real-estate-empire-caters-to-brands-like-hyundai-and-hm/) — shot in Prague; the image is part of The Forbes Collection on Getty Images.
- [Samir Mane](https://www.forbes.com/sites/giacomotognini/2025/04/19/how-this-refugee-became-albanias-first-billionaire/) — Albania's first billionaire, April 2025.
- [Alex Bouzari, DDN](https://www.forbes.com/sites/iainmartin/2026/08/06/ais-latest-rocket-ship-is-an-old-school-28-year-old-data-company/) — August 2026.

Those portraits travelled on to Forbes México, Forbes Česko, Forbes Italia and [Forbes España](https://forbes.es/empresas/682618/remon-vos-el-multimillonario-que-ha-creado-un-imperio-inmobiliario-en-europa/).

## Convoys, dunes and summits

Five Gumball 3000 rallies, with his pictures in the official Gumball 3000 photo book. The Dakar Rally in 2020. Porsche performance cars on frozen lakes in Swedish Lapland. A brief stop at the Singapore night race in 2024. And Kilimanjaro in 2022 — on foot, to Uhuru Peak at 5,895 metres.

Speed teaches the same lesson every time: pick your spot early, read the light, and let your hands do the rest.

## The Playboy Latvia years

Four years, fifty issues: that was Playboy in Latvian, from 2010 to 2014. Andrejs Zavadskis shot twenty-two of those issues — more than any other photographer who worked for the magazine. One of his Latvian models was chosen by a foreign Playboy edition, a first for the Latvian magazine, and in the end three of his models appeared abroad. Madara Palameika's July 2012 cover, below, is one of his. ## First frames

In 2004 a photograph of fireworks over the Riga Radio and Television Tower won first prize in a competition held by the Latvian State Radio and Television Centre. The Riga Photo Club followed, then a Master Craftsman's Diploma from the Latvian Chamber of Crafts in 2008. In 2009 the UK magazine South Wales YOURWEDDING gave him a cover and a feature, and the Latvian Wedding Photographers Association named him Wedding Photographer of the Year. In March 2025 he placed third in the Fstoppers international contest "This Was Difficult!".

## Now

He works from one photo studio in Riga, speaks at Riga Photo Show, has written a photography blog since 2008 and records YouTube gear reviews with the RAW files included. He gave more than 7,000 volunteer photographs to Latvia's 2025 School Youth Song and Dance Festival. In 2026 he launched [ProPortfolio.pro](https://proportfolio.pro/?utm_source=readthedocs&utm_medium=article&utm_campaign=bio) — a free platform for photographers, videographers, models, stylists and studios.
